_If you're excited about the opportunity to contribute to the digital safety of millions, to work alongside a team of curious, driven individuals, and to solve hard problems in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, then we want to hear from you. As part of 1Password’s Developer Relations team, you’ll grow our presence where developers learn, share, and connect—especially on X, Reddit, and other relevant online communities. You’ll create technically accurate content, build relationships with technical creators, support product launches and events, and turn developer conversations into measurable audience growth and product interest. Your work will help advance 1Password’s mission to unleash employee productivity without compromising security. Reporting to the Director of Developer Relations, this role is remote within the United States, with the San Francisco Bay Area preferred.

**How we’re using AI today**

Our Engineering, Product, and Design teams are thoughtfully integrating AI across the full software and product development lifecycle to move faster without sacrificing quality or security. In practice, that looks like engineers using AI-assisted coding tools to accelerate reviews and catch bugs earlier, product managers synthesizing user research at scale, and designers rapidly prototyping and iterating with AI-generated mockups. We approach AI the same way we approach security: with clear principles, human accountability at every consequential decision point, and rigorous evaluation before anything ships to customers.

This is a remote opportunity within Canada and the US.

**What we're looking for:**

• Experience in developer marketing, technical social media, community growth, or developer relations.

• A track record of growing a developer or technical audience, with a clear understanding of your contribution and results.

• Strong writing skills and the ability to turn technical ideas into clear, engaging content.

• Technical fluency, including the ability to follow developer documentation, try tools or integrations, and collaborate comfortably with engineers.

• Experience coordinating creator partnerships or marketing campaigns involving multiple contributors, deadlines, and deliverables.

• Preferred: Experience marketing developer tools, security products, infrastructure, or artificial intelligence (AI) tools; an existing network of developer creators and community builders; or experience with employee advocacy, paid social experiments, or campaign attribution.

**What you can expect:**

• Grow 1Password’s developer audience through campaigns and ongoing engagement across X, Reddit, and other relevant channels.

• Create posts, threads, and short-form content about developer workflows, product capabilities, security, and AI agents, partnering with engineers and developer advocates to ensure accuracy.

• Identify and build relationships with technical creators, coordinate campaign briefs and deliverables, manage approved budgets, and track performance.

• Support product launches with creator partnerships and social content that help developers understand new capabilities and get started.

• Build an employee ambassador program that helps engineers share technical insights, projects, and lessons learned in their own voices.

• Participate in developer communities by contributing useful answers, identifying relevant conversations, and bringing developer feedback back to the team.

• Amplify developer events, experiment with new formats and messaging, and use engagement, qualified traffic, and attributable signups to improve the program and connect campaigns to product adoption.
