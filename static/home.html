How it works

Three steps, no guesswork.

01

Upload what you have

A plain resume works as it is: PDF, .txt or .md. A master profile with [V] and [NO] tags is stricter, because lines you mark [NO] never count.

02

Every posting, read the same way

Requirements are pulled from each description and weighted: 3 for must-haves, 2 for required skills, 1 for nice-to-haves.

03

A score you can check

Each job shows the evidence it matched and the gaps it found. The score is the share of the posting's weight your profile backs, so a 78 tells you exactly what is behind it.

What makes it different

Built to be trusted.

Evidence, not keywords
A skill counts only when a line of your profile supports it. Knowing Docker never implies Kubernetes, and AWS never implies Azure.
Rules, not a language model
Scoring is deterministic: the same profile and posting always give the same score, and nothing you upload is sent to an AI service.
Your profile stays yours
It is stored with your account and used only to score jobs for you. Replace it, or delete your account and everything in it, whenever you like.

Questions

What is a master profile?

A Markdown file of everything you have done, one fact per line, each tagged [V] when you can back it up or [NO] when you must never claim it. VouchStudio treats it as the source of truth, so it scores more strictly than a plain resume.

Who can see my resume?

Only you. It is kept with your account, used to score the board for you, and never shown to other accounts. Deleting your account deletes it.

Does it write my resume for me?

With Pro, yes. From a tagged master profile with a resume kit, VouchStudio picks the bullets that best cover each job, builds a one-page PDF and checks it. Every line traces back to a fact you marked as verified, so it never claims something you have not done.

Which jobs are on the board?

Public postings from company job boards (Greenhouse, Ashby and others) across the United States and Canada, refreshed regularly. Listings without a full description are shown but not scored.
